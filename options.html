Google Chat to Telegram

Leave chat.google.com open in Chrome. The tab can stay in the background. New messages are forwarded, including in the chat you already have open. Messages already on screen when the watcher starts are not sent.

Telegram

  1. Open @BotFather, send /newbot, and paste the token above.
  2. Open your new bot and send it any message.
  3. Click Find chat id, then Save.
  4. Click Send test.
